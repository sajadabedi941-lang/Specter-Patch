AAB heavy UNIT_BUILD buttons visible but grey (not purchasable)
==============================================================
Root cause:
America_AdvancedAirBase used Revolution ABAirF2 ExtraPublicBones in a 2x3
layout (Runway1Parking1-3 / Runway2Parking1-3) while ParkingPlaceBehavior was
NumRows=1 NumCols=6 (stock US_AirField expects Runway1Parking1..Runway6Parking1).
Parking slots never resolved -> UNIT_BUILD stays disabled/grey.

Fix:
1) AAB DefaultConditionState -> US_AirField with stock 1x6 ExtraPublicBones
2) Enlarge AAB Scale=1.50, geometry, ApproachHeight=140 (aircraft Scale/models unchanged)
3) Heavies-only CommandSet (fighters excluded)
4) Simplify B-2/B-52/E-3/C-17/KC-135/AC-130 behaviors
5) ZZZZ CommandButton unlock (no Science/Rank/Upgrade/Prerequisite)
