  Remapped to AuroraBomberVoice*.
- DamageFX AAHitscanDamageFX -> None (not in Specter)

- FXOnGroundDeath FX_BigMissileCarrierDeath -> FX_JetOnGroundDeath (Specter)
- B2Falling sound omitted from FX_B2DeathInitial (asset absent)
