SPECTER - Europe Airbase Structure Fix
======================================

Install: copy these files into the Specter Data folder (replace existing):
  _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
  _SPEC_ART_ONE.big

Keep EnglishZH.big and AudioZH.big unchanged.

Nuclear/Atomic building (America LGM-30) is restored on dozer slot 12 for
France, Germany, Italy, and the United Kingdom.

No extra helicopter building. Helicopters are built from existing airbases.

Fighter Airbase:
  fighters + attack helicopters

Heavy Airbase:
  transports, AWACS, drones, bombers, remaining helicopters

France Fighter: 8 jets + Tiger HAD
France Heavy: C-130, E-3, NH90, Caracal

Germany Fighter: 10 jets + Tiger UHT
Germany Heavy: A400M, C-130J, E-3, Heron TP, NH90, CH-53, H145M

Italy Fighter: 10 jets + AW249 + A129 Mangusta
Italy Heavy: C-130J, C-27J, G550 CAEW, MQ-9, NH90, AW101, AW139

UK Fighter: 10 jets + Apache AH-64E
UK Heavy: A400M, C-17, E-7, MQ-9 Reaper, Vulcan, Chinook, Merlin, Wildcat, Puma

Aircraft objects, ART, and weapons are unchanged from the Europe air-force pack.
Russia, China, and other countries are not modified.

Packer: patch/tools/big/pack_europe_airbase_structure.py
