SPECTER - Europe Air Force Expansion (Germany / Italy / United Kingdom)
=======================================================================

Install: copy these files into the Specter Data folder (replace existing):
  _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
  _SPEC_ART_ONE.big

Keep EnglishZH.big and AudioZH.big unchanged.

Does not change Russia, China, or France air forces.

Aircraft with no unique mesh keep the closest donor model. Gameplay names
are the real aircraft only. Donor mesh names never appear on buttons or CSF.
