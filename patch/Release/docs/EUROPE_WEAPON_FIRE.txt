SPECTER - Europe Aircraft Weapon Fire Fix
=========================================

Install: copy these files into the Specter Data folder (replace existing):
  _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
  _SPEC_ART_ONE.big
  INSTALL.txt is in the ZIP for reference.

Keep EnglishZH.big and AudioZH.big unchanged.

ONLY France, Germany, Italy, and the United Kingdom air units are patched.
Russia, China, USA, and other countries are not modified.
Airbase menus and ART are unchanged from europe-airbase-structure-v1.

What was broken:
  Overlay weapons pointed at missing projectile objects
  (France_MICA_Projectile, France_SCALP_Projectile, France_Meteor_Projectile,
  France_Projectile_Meteor_Rafale). Those weapons could not fire.

Fixes:
  All FR/DE/IT/UK air weapons retargeted to packed projectiles:
    Meteor / MICA / IRIS-T / ASRAAM / AMRAAM -> MeteorMissile_Object
    SCALP / Taurus / Storm Shadow / Exocet   -> Kh59MK2_Object
    AASM / Paveway / JDAM / bombs            -> Fab-250
    Hellfire / Brimstone / ATGM              -> AGM114L_MissileObject
    Rockets                                  -> GenericUnguidedRockets
    Cannon                                   -> 30mm_API-T_Projectile
  ClipSize + ClipReloadTime present on every air weapon.
  Missiles AutoReloadsClip = RETURN_TO_BASE.
  Cannons and rockets AutoReloadsClip = Yes.
  Combat jets: cannon TERTIARY, AutoChooseSources, AutoAcquireEnemiesWhenIdle,
  OutOfAmmoDamagePerSecond = 0%.
  Attack helis keep cannon + ATGM + rockets (Tiger HAD / UHT, AW249, A129, Apache).
  Transports (A400M, C-130, C-17, NH90, Caracal, CH-53, Chinook, Merlin, Wildcat)
  have no weapons.
  AWACS (E-3, G550 CAEW, E-7) radar only: REVEALS_ENEMY_PATHS, no CAN_ATTACK,
  C17GlobalMasterCommandSet (no FireMainWeapon).

Packer: patch/tools/big/pack_europe_weapon_fire.py
Base BIG: /tmp/europe_airbase_structure

Parser: overlay INI LF/End pass.
Weapon fire validate: every combat WeaponSet slot exists in Weapon.ini and
every ProjectileObject exists as a packed Object.
China/Russia CommandSets unchanged. ART hash unchanged. ZIP extract verify pass.
