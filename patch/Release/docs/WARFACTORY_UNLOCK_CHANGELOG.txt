SPECTER WAR FACTORY START-UNLOCK
================================

Destination countries already had the copied War Factory structure.
Those bars still used reference-country Objects that require NATO
strategy centers, Russia ranks, Egypt palace/industry, Iraq radar,
and USA strategy-center sciences. Dest players do not have those.

This pass unlocks dest factories only:

- Live dest CommandSets in CommandSet.ini / CommandSet_Pakistan.ini
  now use dest-only unlocked buttons for previously locked units.
- New dest-only objects WFUnlock_* have empty Prerequisites.
- Japan / Vietnam / South Korea WarFactory CommandSetUpgrade modules
  (Upgrade_Irq_Tier1/2/3) were removed.
- Protected country objects, CommandButtons, and CommandSets were
  not edited.
