SPECTER — country-specific supply delivery income
================================================

Place 0_SPECTER_CountrySupplyIncome.big in the same folder as generals.exe,
together with:

  0_SPECTER_NatoSpainDisplay.big
  0_SPECTER_CountrySupplyIncome.big
  _SPEC_ART_ONE.big
  _SPEC_ART_TWO.big
  _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big

Do not put this BIG inside Data\ or Art\. Do not rename the 0_ prefix.
Zero Hour registers archives with overwrite=FALSE (first registered internal
path wins). The 0_ name loads before INIZH.big and _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big.

What it changes
---------------
GameData.ValuePerSupplyBox = 25 (global SAGE crate value).
Each of the 25 playable countries dumps MaxBoxes * 25 dollars for one full
equivalent gatherer load. Spawned trucks/chinooks are country-unique so a
change for USA does not change Spain, Russia, or China.

Map supply piles keep previous dollar totals (StartingBoxes * 3).
Unique faction workers keep $75 trips (MaxBoxes 1 -> 3).

This overlay does not change missiles, ART archives, or building art.
Supply income passed static validation. In-game Zero Hour runtime testing
has not been performed.
