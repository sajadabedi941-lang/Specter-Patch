SPECTER — global 300-second missile re-fire cooldown and heavy-warhead radii

Put these files in ONE folder. Do not rename them.

- _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
- _SPEC_ART_ONE.big
- 0_SPECTER_MissileCooldown.big
- Launch_Specter.bat
- Install_SpecterPatch.bat

Run Install_SpecterPatch.bat once, or Launch_Specter.bat to play.
Both copy the BIG files beside generals.exe. Do not put BIGs inside Data or Art.
Do not rename 0_SPECTER_MissileCooldown.big to zz_.

This overlay owns Data\INI\Weapon.ini, the Data\INI\Weapon\ last-wins
fragments, and Data\English\generals.csf. It sets DelayBetweenShots = 300000
on every factory A-J PRIMARY weapon and scales splash / shock / detonation FX
/ Death HACK radius for factory missiles whose configured warhead mass exceeds
4000 kg.

Production-button tooltips show Re-fire: 300 sec on all 250 factory missiles
and Blast radius from the live PrimaryDamageRadius on the 16 missiles above
4000 kg. Warhead kg, range, accuracy, and radar-stealth lines are unchanged
where they already matched last-wins DATA.

Zero Hour was not launched while this package was built.
