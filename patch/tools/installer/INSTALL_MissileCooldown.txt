SPECTER — global 300-second missile re-fire cooldown and heavy-warhead radii

Put these files in ONE folder. Do not rename them.

- _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
- _SPEC_ART_ONE.big
- 0_SPECTER_MissileCooldown.big
- Launch_Specter.bat
- Install_SpecterPatch.bat

Run Install_SpecterPatch.bat once, or Launch_Specter.bat to play.
Both copy the BIG files beside generals.exe. Do not put BIGs inside Data or Art.
Do not rename 0_SPECTER_MissileCooldown.big to zz_.

This overlay owns Data\INI\Weapon.ini and the Data\INI\Weapon\ last-wins
fragments. It sets DelayBetweenShots = 300000 on every factory A-J PRIMARY
weapon and scales splash / shock / detonation FX / Death HACK radius for
factory missiles whose configured warhead mass exceeds 4000 kg.

Zero Hour was not launched while this package was built.
