SPECTER — remove Spy Satellite from USA, Israel, and Iraq radar menus
حذف دکمهٔ عمومی Spy Satellite از رادار آمریکا، اسرائیل و عراق

Put these files in ONE folder. Do not rename them.

- _SPEC_DATA_ONE.big
- _SPEC_ART_ONE.big
- 0_SPECTER_USAIsraelIraqRadar.big
- Launch_Specter.bat
- Install_SpecterPatch.bat

Run Install_SpecterPatch.bat once, or Launch_Specter.bat to play.
Both copy the BIG files beside generals.exe. Do not put BIGs inside Data or Art.
Do not rename 0_SPECTER_USAIsraelIraqRadar.big to zz_.

This overlay owns Data\INI\CommandSet.ini and only changes
AmericaRadarStationCommandSet, IsraelRadarStationCommandSet, and
Iraq_RadarStationCommandSet. Other countries keep the Specter DATA menus.

Zero Hour was not launched while this package was built.
